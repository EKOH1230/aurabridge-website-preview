How to use

  1. Fill the reservoir with serum or essential oil and close the cap.
  2. Press to dispense a small amount onto the roller-ball tip.
  3. Roll the tip along the scalp.
  4. Wipe the tip after use.

Rinse removable parts with water if needed, then dry them before storing. Do not soak the device.
