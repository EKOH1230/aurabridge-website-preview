# AuraBridge website

An English B2B website for AuraBridge. It introduces the company and shows three products in the range: two beauty devices and one alternating-pressure air mattress.

**View the website:** https://ekoh1230.github.io/aurabridge-website-preview/

## View locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. The site uses plain HTML, CSS, and a small JavaScript file, with no build step or dependencies.

## Pages

- `index.html` — introduction
- `about.html` — purpose and principles
- `partnerships.html` — potential areas for discussion
- `products.html` — three models in the AuraBridge range, beauty devices first and the air mattress last, with locally hosted photos
- `contact.html` — company address, email, and phone

Shared assets live in `assets/`. Product photo sources are recorded in `docs/product-images.json`. The header mark is `assets/aurabridge-logo.png`, the original bridge sketch with heavier strokes. The favicon uses the same bridge.

## Content status

The site is in English, with Simplified Chinese, French, German, and Spanish copies under `zh/`, `fr/`, `de/`, and `es/`. The product list is KH-2406, KH-2307 and OB-1600. Beauty devices and the air mattress are presented as the AuraBridge range, with no supplier names on the public pages. Photos are stored locally and recorded in `docs/product-images.json`. KH-2406 uses six factory photos, with the front render as the main image. Each product has a detail page. KH-2307 reservoir capacity and weight are listed as available on request. Prices are not shown. Certification and treatment claims are not used. No contact form, tracking, payment, or data collection is connected.

Publishing this repository with GitHub Pages makes the site publicly accessible to anyone with the URL. Keep internal source documents out of this repository.
