# AuraBridge website preview

An English B2B website preview for AuraBridge. It introduces the proposed positioning and shows four selected products: three beauty devices and one alternating-pressure air mattress. Availability, contact details, and specific commercial services remain under review.

**View the website:** https://ekoh1230.github.io/aurabridge-website-preview/

## View locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. The site uses plain HTML, CSS, and a small JavaScript file, with no build step or dependencies.

## Pages

- `index.html` — introduction
- `about.html` — purpose and principles
- `partnerships.html` — potential areas for discussion
- `products.html` — four selected models, beauty devices first and the air mattress last, with locally hosted photos
- `contact.html` — contact placeholder

Shared assets live in `assets/`. Product photo sources are recorded in `docs/product-images.json`. The logo is a temporary image provided for the project and may be replaced when the final mark is approved.

## Content status

This is a review draft. The product list is four models approved for the preview: KF-2212, KH-2406, KH-2307 and OB-1600. Cards use locally hosted factory photos recorded in `docs/product-images.json`. One KH-2406 photo was left out because its title contains the characters 米诺. Prices are not shown; each card links to a request-a-quote path on the contact page. Certification and treatment claims are not used. Before treating the products as available through AuraBridge, confirm the legal entity and commercial role, regional availability, specifications and regulatory documentation, permission for commercial use of factory media, actual partnership capabilities, and public contact details. No contact form, tracking, payment, or data collection is connected.

Publishing this repository with GitHub Pages makes the preview publicly accessible to anyone with the URL. Keep internal source documents out of this repository.
